#  RHOAIUX Story Point Scale

Story points are used to estimate effort, complexity, and risk. They reflect how much work a task requires, regardless of who is doing it. This shared understanding helps the team align and plan sprints more effectively.

Helpful or related document(s): [Guide to Add a Sprint and Story Points to a RHOAIUX Jira Ticket](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yPi5kuNplKOZ_TUJiCRhYRP5VV0QcGWBc8ZRHplSewk/edit?usp=sharing).

---

### How to add story points to a Jira ticket

To estimate effort for sprint planning, designers should assign story points using the Story Points field.

1. Open your Jira ticket.  
2. Click Edit.  
3. Scroll down and locate the Story Points field in the Field Tab tab.   
4. Enter your estimate using this scale..  
5. Click Update to save.

---

| Story Point Estimate | Definition | Effort and Complexity | Examples |
| ----- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | An extremely straightforward task.  | Very low effort, no risk.  | Update spacing, fix a typo, export assets |
| 2 | Straightforward work, little risk, little to no investigation needed.  | Low effort, clear solution. Low risk. | Tweak wireframes, create a simple user flow. |
| 3 | Doesn’t have to be complex but can be time consuming. Fairly straightforward work, some minor risks. Some collaboration or iteration might be needed. | Moderate effort, low risk.  | Draft a new screen, update a pattern, prepare for a review. |
| 5 | Probably requires some investigation, discussions, and collaboration with others. Time consuming or complex. There can be risks involved.  | High effort or moderate complexity. | Create a new user experience flow. Conduct user interviews and synthesize the findings. |
| 8 | Big task that requires investigation, collaboration with others \+ discussion. Solution can be quite challenging. Risks are expected. This ticket may also need or should be broken down into smaller, more achievable tickets.  | Large, complex, or unclear. High risk. | Full pattern definition. Multi-feature research. |
| 13 | Too big: split it up. Ideally this shouldn’t be used. If an issue is considered this big, it should be split into smaller tasks that follow the ranking above. | Not sprint-sized. | Break down before pointing. |

## Story Point Adjustments for PTO / Holidays

| Missed Days from Sprint | Story Point Adjustments |
| ----- | :---- |
| half day | Minus 2 story points |
| 1 day | Minus 3 story points |
| 2 \- 3 days | Minus 5 story points |
| 4 \- 6 days | Minus 8 story points |
| 3 weeks (full sprint) | Minus 20 story points |

This is a rough summary of how story point goals are adjusted when there is PTO, holiday adjustments or other days away from productive sprint work. These are subject to be adjusted further based on the individual scenario as it makes sense, the below should be used just as a starting point.
