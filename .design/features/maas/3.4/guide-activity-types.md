# Activity Types for UX product work  

---

# The goal 

Our goal is to assess and monitor UX maturity across our product areas by tracking UX phases in Jira. We've established a set of UX activity types for this purpose.

We hypothesize that a healthy product area will show a diverse mix of activity types across the entire Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)—from the initial Orient and Explore phases to the later Make and Monitor phases. A lack of breadth in these activity  types could also indicate a limited investment in a particular area.

# The pilot 

## What’s expected

Open questions for RHOAI UXD Managers to monitor: 

* The consult activity type has been a conversation in the past, does this come up still?

**Bolded phases \*** Following a recent retrospective with UX RHAI managers, our hypothesis is that the existing phases can be consolidated into four core stages: Orient, Explore, Make, and Monitor (which is a subset of the design process in the table below). This is a hypothesis and should not limit usage of any of the available activity types.
